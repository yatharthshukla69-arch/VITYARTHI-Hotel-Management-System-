## Problem Statement

Hotels need to maintain guest details, room availability, reservations, check-in/check-out information, and billing records. When these activities are handled through separate manual records, it can become difficult to search, update, and maintain consistent information.

The Hotel Management System is proposed as a centralized Java-based application for organizing these common hotel-management activities.

## Scope of the Project

The project covers:

- Guest/customer record management
- Room information and availability
- Reservation creation, modification, and cancellation
- Check-in and check-out
- Billing and charge calculation
- Search and update operations
- Validation and error handling
- Basic reporting/record viewing

## Target Users

- Hotel receptionist/front-desk staff
- Hotel administrator
- Reservation staff
- Billing/front-office staff

## High-Level Features

1. Guest Management
2. Room Management
3. Reservation Management
4. Check-in and Check-out
5. Billing Management
6. User Management, where implemented
7. Validation and Error Handling
8. Reporting / Record Viewing

## Expected Outcome

The system is expected to provide an organized workflow for common hotel operations while demonstrating modular Java programming, record management, validation, testing, documentation, and version control.
